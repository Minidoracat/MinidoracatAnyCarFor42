description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Bring a welding mask and a propane torch with enough uses left
description=[*] Right-click a vehicle and choose "Dismantle Vehicle"
description=[*] If items are still inside, you are asked to confirm first
description=[*] When it finishes the vehicle is gone and the materials drop on the ground
description=[/olist]

description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Dismantle any vehicle[/b]: Intact cars and trailers get a "Dismantle Vehicle" option
description=[*] [b]Wrecks stay vanilla[/b]: Burnt and smashed vehicles keep the vanilla option
description=[*] [b]Works on multiplayer servers[/b]: The server re-checks before removing the vehicle and giving materials
description=[*] [b]Items warning[/b]: A confirmation appears when items are still inside the vehicle
description=[*] [b]Loot multiplier[/b]: A sandbox option scales the materials you get
description=[*] [b]Dismantle log[/b]: The server records who dismantled which vehicle and where
